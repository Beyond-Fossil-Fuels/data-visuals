Share of Europe's electricity generation each month. Gas is split into the three largest gas generators over the period and all other countries. Hover over the chart for the figures.

Source: Ember monthly electricity data, as compiled for Beyond Fossil Fuels' quarterly reports | Last update: September 2026

Note: Europe covers the EU27, the UK, Norway, Switzerland, Türkiye, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and Serbia. Albania, Kosovo, Moldova, North Macedonia and Ukraine are not included, as their monthly data is incomplete. Missing recent months for Malta, Switzerland and Türkiye are filled from national sources. Other fossil fuels are mainly coal; other sources are nuclear, hydro, bioenergy and other renewables.
